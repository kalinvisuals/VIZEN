# Privacy Policy — VIZEN and VIZEN Connector

*Last updated: October 18, 2026*

VIZEN (the Windows app) and VIZEN Connector (the browser extension for Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome and Brave) are
made by kalinvisuals. This policy explains what data they handle. In short: **everything stays on your PC. Nothing is
sent to us or to anyone else.**

## VIZEN Connector (browser extension)

**What it reads.** For each open tab in a normal (not incognito) window: the tab title, the site name (host name only,
for example `youtube.com`, never the full address), whether the tab is playing sound or is muted, and whether it is the
active tab. Incognito / InPrivate tabs are never read.

**Where it goes.** Only to the VIZEN app on the same PC, through the browser's native messaging feature, which works
only on the local computer. The extension makes no network requests and has no servers. No data is sold, shared,
transferred or used for advertising, analytics or profiling.

**What it does with it.** VIZEN shows your tabs as sources and lets you switch to a tab, mute or unmute it, or move it
into its own window. These actions happen only when you ask for them.

**Live mirror.** When you choose "Mirror this tab live", the extension uses the browser's tab capture feature to show
that tab in a small window on your PC, so VIZEN can capture it like any window. The picture and sound are not recorded
or sent anywhere. Capture starts only after your click and stops when you close the window.

**Permissions.** `tabs` (tab titles and sites), `nativeMessaging` (talk to the VIZEN app on your PC) and `tabCapture`
(the live mirror, only after your click). The extension has no access to page content and adds no scripts to web pages.

**Storage.** The extension stores nothing. VIZEN keeps only the current list of tabs in memory while it runs.

## VIZEN (Windows app)

VIZEN has no account, no telemetry and no analytics. Your settings, notes, drawings, profiles and media library list
are saved only on your PC, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\VIZEN`. Web pages that you open yourself in a VIZEN browser source load
from the internet like in any browser.

## Removing your data

Uninstall the extension from your browser and VIZEN from *Settings → Apps*. To remove VIZEN's saved settings, delete
the folder `%LOCALAPPDATA%\VIZEN`. In VIZEN, **Disconnect** on the Browser page removes the link between VIZEN and the
extension.

## Children

VIZEN and VIZEN Connector do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
